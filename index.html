Global Bike Sales Analysis Pipeline

An end-to-end pipeline analyzing global bike sales data (Kaggle, 2013–2023, 33 columns): data stored in PostgreSQL, processed with Apache Airflow ETL, validated with Great Expectations, indexed into Elasticsearch, and visualized in Kibana dashboards.

  • The US & Australia are the most profitable markets
  • Ages 25–64 generate 86% of revenue
  • Discounts show diminishing returns beyond 10–20%

Bird Species Classification

Bird species image classification with MobileNetV2 (transfer learning, TensorFlow/Keras) on a Kaggle dataset, then improved through a refined model. Includes a demo on Hugging Face Spaces.

100% accuracy on test data
  • Recognizes species across varied poses & backgrounds
  • Applications suggested: conservation, education, ecotourism
